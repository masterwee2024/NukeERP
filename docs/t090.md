# T089 — Recruitment (Basic)

- **Phase**: 7
- **Depends On**: T010
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
Job requisition → approval. Job posting details. Candidate application tracking. Interview scheduling & feedback. Offer letter generation (template merge). Onboarding checklist.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/hrm/models.py`):
  - `JobRequisition`: title, department FK, designation FK, vacancies, salary_range, justification, status (draft/approved/open/closed), approval_request FK, company FK
  - `Candidate`: name, email, phone, resume, source (website/referral/agency), status (new/screening/interview/offer/hired/rejected), company FK
  - `Interview`: candidate FK, job_requisition FK, interviewer FK, scheduled_date, feedback, rating, status (scheduled/completed/cancelled)
  - `OfferLetter`: candidate FK, job_requisition FK, offer_date, start_date, salary, status (draft/sent/accepted/rejected), company FK
  - `OnboardingChecklist`: employee FK (after hire), items (JSONField — [{task, completed, assigned_to, due_date}])
- **Service**: CRUD, approval, interview scheduling, offer generation, onboarding tracking
- **API**: Full CRUD for all entities
