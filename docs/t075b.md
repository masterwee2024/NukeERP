# T075b — Direct / Indirect Cost Classification

- **Phase**: 6
- **Depends On**: T075a
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
Cost classification system for manufacturing — separating direct costs (material, labour, overhead directly attributable to product) from indirect costs (factory overhead, administrative overhead). Cost classification drives absorption costing and marginal costing approaches.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/mrp/models.py`):
  - `CostClassification`: id, name (direct_material/direct_labour/direct_overhead/indirect_material/indirect_labour/indirect_overhead/admin_overhead/selling_overhead), gl_account FK, description, company FK, is_active
  - `ProductCostStructure`: id, item FK, direct_material_cost, direct_labour_cost, direct_overhead_cost, indirect_material_cost, indirect_labour_cost, indirect_overhead_cost, total_cost, period FK, company FK
- **Service** (`apps/mrp/services/cost_classification_service.py`):
  - `classify_cost(cost_type, amount, work_order_id)` — assign cost to classification
  - `get_direct_costs(item_id, period)` — sum of all direct costs
  - `get_indirect_costs(item_id, period)` — sum of all indirect costs
  - `get_cost_structure(item_id, period)` — full cost breakdown by classification
  - `build_cost_structure_from_wo(work_order_id)` — aggregate from WO costs

### Cost Classification Hierarchy
```
Manufacturing Cost
├── Direct Costs (traced to specific product)
│   ├── Direct Material (raw materials in BOM)
│   ├── Direct Labour (production worker hours × rate)
│   └── Direct Overhead (machine depreciation, power for specific machine)
└── Indirect Costs (allocated, not traced to specific product)
    ├── Indirect Material (lubricants, cleaning supplies)
    ├── Indirect Labour (supervisor, maintenance)
    ├── Indirect Overhead (factory rent, insurance, general depreciation)
    ├── Administrative Overhead (office costs allocated to production)
    └── Selling Overhead (marketing, sales commission)
```

### Frontend (React)
- **Cost Classification Setup**: configure cost types and GL accounts
- **Cost Structure Report**: direct vs indirect breakdown per item
- **Cost Structure by Period**: trend analysis
