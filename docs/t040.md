# T039 — Cash Management

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T018
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
Cash accounts ledger linked to bank GL accounts. Cash position dashboard showing real-time balances across all accounts. Cash transfer between accounts.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Service** (`apps/treasury/services/cash_service.py`):
  - `get_cash_position(company)` — sum of all bank account balances
  - `transfer_cash(from_account_id, to_account_id, amount, date, reference)` — create transfer JE (DR bank, CR bank)
- **API**: cash position, transfer, account list

### Frontend (React)
- `CashPositionDashboard` — real-time balance cards per bank account, total position
- `CashTransferForm` — transfer between accounts
