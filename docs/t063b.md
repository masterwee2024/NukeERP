# T063b — Consignment Sales

- **Phase**: 5
- **Depends On**: T063, T062
- **Estimated Effort**: 3 days

## What Is Being Built
Consignment sales management — goods sent to customer but ownership remains with seller until sold. Track consignment stock at customer location. Invoicing on sell-through. Consignment stock report. Reconciliation of consignment inventory.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/crm/models.py`):
  - `ConsignmentOrder`: id, order_number, customer FK, warehouse FK, status (draft/delivered/selling/completed/cancelled), total_value, company FK, created_at, expected_return_date
  - `ConsignmentOrderLine`: order FK, item FK, quantity_sent, quantity_sold, quantity_returned, uom FK, unit_price, total_value
  - `ConsignmentSettlement`: id, settlement_number, consignment_order FK, period, quantity_sold, amount_invoiced, amount_paid, status (draft/settled/paid), company FK, created_at
- **Service** (`apps/crm/services/consignment_service.py`):
  - `create_consignment_order(data)` — create order with line items
  - `dispatch_consignment(order_id)` — stock movement out (consignment type), no GL revenue yet
  - `report_sales(order_id, sales_data)` — record quantities sold by customer
  - `settle_consignment(order_id, period)` — generate invoice for sold quantities
  - `return_unsold(order_id)` — stock movement back for unsold goods
  - `get_consignment_stock(customer_id)` — stock at customer location
- **GL Entries**:
  ```
  Dispatch to consignment:
  DR Consignment Stock (asset)       XXX
  CR Inventory                       XXX

  On settlement (customer sells):
  DR Accounts Receivable              XXX
  CR Revenue                         XXX
  DR Cost of Goods Sold              XXX
  CR Consignment Stock (asset)       XXX

  Return of unsold goods:
  DR Inventory                       XXX
  CR Consignment Stock (asset)       XXX
  ```

### Frontend (React)
- **Consignment Order Form**: create order, dispatch
- **Consignment Stock Report**: stock at each customer
- **Settlement Form**: record sales, generate invoice
- **Return Form**: return unsold goods
