# T065b — Supplier Portal

- **Phase**: 5
- **Depends On**: T065c, T053, T052, T019a
- **Estimated Effort**: 5 days

## What Is Being Built
Supplier portal for vendors to self-service: view purchase orders, acknowledge POs, submit invoices, respond to RFQs, update delivery schedule, view payment status, collaborate with procurement team via dedicated messaging channel. Desktop and mobile responsive.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/core/models.py`):
  - `PortalUser`: (shared with T065a — entity_type='supplier', entity_id → Vendor)
  - `SupplierInvoiceSubmission`: id, portal_user FK, supplier FK, invoice_number, invoice_date, invoice_file (PDF), total_amount, status (submitted/validated/approved/rejected), validated_by FK, notes, company FK, created_at
  - `SupplierInvoiceLine`: submission FK, item FK, description, quantity, unit_price, amount
  - `DeliveryUpdate`: id, purchase_order FK, portal_user FK, expected_date, tracking_number, carrier, notes, status (on_track/delayed), updated_at
  - `RFQResponseSupplier`: id, rfq FK, portal_user FK, response_date, total_amount, notes, status (draft/submitted), company FK, created_at
  - `RFQResponseLineSupplier`: response FK, rfq_line FK, unit_price, lead_time_days, notes

- **Service** (`apps/portal/services/supplier_portal_service.py`):
  - `get_supplier_documents(vendor_id)` — POs, GRNs, payments, statements
  - `acknowledge_po(po_id, vendor_id)` — acknowledge receipt of PO
  - `submit_invoice(portal_user_id, data)` — submit supplier invoice
  - `update_delivery(po_id, data)` — update delivery schedule
  - `submit_rfq_response(rfq_id, data)` — respond to RFQ
  - `get_payment_status(vendor_id)` — payment status and history
  - `get_messaging_channel(portal_user_id)` — dedicated procurement channel

- **API** (`apps/portal/api/supplier_api.py`):
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/purchase-orders/` — supplier's POs
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/purchase-orders/{id}/` — PO detail
  - `POST /api/v1/portal/supplier/purchase-orders/{id}/acknowledge/` — acknowledge PO
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/grns/` — supplier's GRNs
  - `POST /api/v1/portal/supplier/invoices/` — submit invoice
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/invoices/` — submitted invoices
  - `PUT /api/v1/portal/supplier/purchase-orders/{id}/delivery/` — update delivery
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/rfqs/` — assigned RFQs
  - `POST /api/v1/portal/supplier/rfqs/{id}/respond/` — submit RFQ response
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/payments/` — payment status
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/statements/` — account statement
  - `GET /api/v1/portal/supplier/messaging/` — procurement channel

- **Security**: All queries scoped to portal_user's vendor_id. No cross-supplier data leakage.

### Frontend (React)
- **SupplierPortalLayout** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierPortalLayout.tsx`):
  - Portal layout (similar to client portal)
  - Sidebar: Dashboard, Purchase Orders, Invoices, RFQs, Deliveries, Payments, Messaging

- **SupplierDashboard** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierDashboard.tsx`):
  - KPI cards: pending POs, outstanding payments, open RFQs
  - Recent activity list

- **SupplierPOPage** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierPOPage.tsx`):
  - PO list with status
  - PO detail with items
  - Acknowledge button
  - Delivery update form

- **SupplierInvoicePage** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierInvoicePage.tsx`):
  - Submit invoice form (upload PDF + line items)
  - Submitted invoices list with status
  - Invoice detail

- **SupplierRFQPage** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierRFQPage.tsx`):
  - RFQ list
  - RFQ detail with items
  - Response form (price, lead time per item)
  - Submit response

- **SupplierPaymentPage** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierPaymentPage.tsx`):
  - Payment status list
  - Account statement
  - Outstanding balance

- **SupplierMessagingPage** (`frontend/src/components/portal/SupplierMessagingPage.tsx`):
  - Dedicated channel with procurement team
  - Same messaging UI as internal
