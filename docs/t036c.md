# T036c — Preventive Maintenance

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T036, T016
- **Estimated Effort**: 4 days

## What Is Being Built
Preventive maintenance scheduling for assets — factory machineries, delivery trucks, equipment. Time-based (every 3 months) or usage-based (every 5,000 hours) scheduling. Auto-generate maintenance work orders. Maintenance calendar. Maintenance checklist per asset type. Internal team or outsourced vendor assignment.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/assets/models.py`):
  - `MaintenanceSchedule`: id, asset FK, schedule_type (time_based/usage_based), frequency_days (for time-based), frequency_hours (for usage-based), last_maintenance_date, last_maintenance_hours, next_maintenance_date, maintenance_type (service/inspection/calibration/overhaul), priority (low/medium/high/critical), estimated_duration_hours, assigned_team FK (optional — internal), vendor FK (optional — outsourced), checklist_template (JSON), is_active, company FK, created_at
  - `MaintenanceChecklistTemplate`: id, name, asset_category FK (optional), items (JSON array of checklist items), company FK
  - `MaintenanceChecklistItem`: checklist_template FK, item_name, description, is_mandatory, sort_order
- **Service** (`apps/assets/services/preventive_maintenance_service.py`):
  - `create_schedule(asset_id, data)` — create maintenance schedule
  - `update_next_date(schedule_id)` — recalculate next maintenance date after completion
  - `get_due_schedules(company)` — schedules due within N days
  - `generate_work_orders(company)` — auto-generate WOs for due schedules
  - `get_maintenance_calendar(company, month)` — calendar view of scheduled maintenance
- **Celery Task**: `check_maintenance_schedules` — runs daily, generates WOs for due schedules
- **API** (`apps/assets/api/preventive_maintenance_api.py`):
  - Schedule CRUD, due schedules, calendar, generate work orders

### Frontend (React)
- **Maintenance Schedule Page**: list of schedules with next due date, status
- **Maintenance Calendar**: monthly view showing scheduled maintenance per asset
- **Schedule Form**: configure frequency, type, assign team/vendor, checklist
- **Due Maintenance Widget**: dashboard widget showing overdue and upcoming

### Maintenance Types by Asset Category
| Asset Category | Typical Schedule | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Factory Machinery | Every 3 months | Service + Inspection |
| Delivery Trucks | Every 10,000 km or 6 months | Service |
| HVAC Systems | Every 6 months | Inspection + Calibration |
| Forklifts | Every 500 hours | Service |
| Computers/IT | Yearly | Inspection |
| Fire Safety Equipment | Yearly | Inspection + Certification |
