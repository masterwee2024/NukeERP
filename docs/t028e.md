# T028e — Cost Centre Accounting

- **Phase**: 2
- **Depends On**: T021
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
Cost centre tracking for management accounting. Assign costs to departments, work centers, or projects. Cost allocation between cost centres. Departmental P&L reports. Budget by cost centre.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/financial/models.py`):
  - `CostCentre`: id, code, name, description, department FK (optional), manager FK (optional), budget_amount, company FK, is_active, created_at
  - `CostCentreAllocation`: id, cost_centre FK, account FK, period FK, amount, allocation_method (direct/percentage/formula), source_cost_centre FK (optional)
- **Service** (`apps/financial/services/cost_centre_service.py`):
  - `assign_cost_to_centre(journal_entry_line_id, cost_centre_id)` — tag GL line to cost centre
  - `allocate_costs(cost_centre_id, period)` — allocate shared costs based on method
  - `get_cost_centre_report(company, period)` — costs by cost centre
  - `get_departmental_pl(company, period)` — P&L by department
  - `get_cost_centre_budget_vs_actual(company, period)` — budget comparison
- **API** (`apps/financial/api/cost_centre_api.py`):
  - Cost centre CRUD, allocation, reports

### Frontend (React)
- **Cost Centre List Page**: all cost centres with budget and actual
- **Cost Centre Report**: costs breakdown by cost centre
- **Departmental P&L**: P&L by department
- **Budget vs Actual by Cost Centre**: comparison report
