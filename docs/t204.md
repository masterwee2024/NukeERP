# T204 — Property Management: Utility Billing

- **Phase**: 2
- **Depends On**: T200, T203, T036e
- **Estimated Effort**: 4 days

## What Is Being Built
Utility billing for tenants — meter reading per unit, billing by formula (per unit/per sqft/flat rate/pro-rata), common area allocation, utility revenue GL. Integration with Facility Management utility meters.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/property/models.py`):
  - `UtilityMeter`: id, unit FK, property FK, utility_type (electricity/water/gas/sewage), meter_number, current_reading, previous_reading, last_reading_date, company FK
  - `UtilityReading`: id, meter FK, reading_date, previous_reading, current_reading, consumption, reading_by FK, notes
  - `UtilityBill`: id, bill_number, unit FK, tenant FK, lease FK, billing_period_start, billing_period_end, utility_type, consumption, rate_per_unit, shared_area_share, subtotal, tax_amount, total, status (draft/sent/paid), invoice FK (optional — linked to rent invoice), company FK
  - `UtilityFormula`: id, property FK, utility_type, formula (per_unit/per_sqft/flat_rate/pro_rata), rate_per_unit, minimum_charge, common_area_allocation (pro_rata_by_area/equal_split), company FK
- **Service**: record readings, calculate bills, generate invoices, common area allocation
- **API**: meter CRUD, reading recording, bill generation, bill list

### Frontend (React)
- **Meter Reading Page**: enter readings for all units
- **Utility Bill Generation**: batch generate from readings
- **Utility Bill List**: all bills with status
- **Utility Report**: consumption and revenue by property/period
