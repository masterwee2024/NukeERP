# T040 — Bank Account Management

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T018
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
`BankAccount` model (bank name, account number, currency, GL account). Multiple accounts per company. Bank balance vs GL reconciliation check.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Model** (`apps/treasury/models.py`):
  - `BankAccount`: bank_name, account_number, account_name, currency, GL_account FK, opening_balance, current_balance, is_active, company FK
- **Service**: CRUD, balance reconciliation check (bank balance vs GL balance)
- **API**: Bank account CRUD + reconciliation check

### Frontend (React)
- `BankAccountListPage` — list with balances
- `BankAccountFormPage` — create/edit

## Deliverables Checklist
