# T201 — Property Management: Tenant Management

- **Phase**: 2
- **Depends On**: T200
- **Estimated Effort**: 3 days

## What Is Being Built
Tenant master — tenant details, company registration (SSM), contact information, credit screening, tenant history (past leases, payments). Tenant portal for viewing invoices and submitting maintenance requests.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/property/models.py`):
  - `Tenant`: id, name, company_name, registration_no (SSM), contact_person, email, phone, address, id_type (ic/passport/company_reg), id_number, credit_check_status, credit_score, status (active/former/blacklisted), notes, company FK, created_at, updated_at, version
  - `TenantScreening`: id, tenant FK, screening_date, credit_check_result, employer_reference, previous_landlord_reference, result (approved/rejected/pending), notes, screened_by FK
  - `TenantDocument`: id, tenant FK, document_type (ic/sss/tenancy_agreement/other), file, description, uploaded_at
- **Service**: CRUD, screening, tenant history, tenant portal
- **API**: Tenant CRUD, screening, documents, tenant portal endpoints

### Frontend (React)
- **Tenant List Page**: all tenants with status, screening status
- **Tenant Form Page**: create/edit with tabs (details, screening, documents, history)
- **Tenant Detail Page**: profile, lease history, payment history, documents
- **Tenant Portal**: customer-facing pages for invoices and maintenance
