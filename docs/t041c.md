# T041c — Investment Tracking

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T041
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
Investment portfolio tracking for fixed deposits, unit trusts, shares, and other financial instruments. Track purchase date, cost, current value, unrealised gain/loss, income (dividends/interest). Investment valuation reports.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/treasury/models.py`):
  - `Investment`: id, reference_number, investment_type (fd/unit_trust/shares/bonds/other), name, issuer, purchase_date, purchase_amount, current_value, currency, income_type (interest/dividend/none), income_rate, maturity_date (optional), gl_account FK, company FK, is_active, created_at
  - `InvestmentValuation`: id, investment FK, valuation_date, market_value, unrealised_gain_loss, source (manual/market_data)
  - `InvestmentIncome`: id, investment FK, income_date, income_amount, income_type (interest/dividend), received (bool), journal_entry FK, company FK
- **Service** (`apps/treasury/services/investment_service.py`):
  - `create_investment(data)` — create investment record
  - `update_valuation(investment_id, market_value, date)` — record valuation
  - `record_income(investment_id, amount, date)` — record income receipt
  - `get_portfolio_summary(company)` — total invested, total value, total gain/loss
  - `get_investment_report(company)` — detailed report by type
- **API**: Investment CRUD, valuation recording, income recording, portfolio summary
