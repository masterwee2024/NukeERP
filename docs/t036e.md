# T036e — Facility Management

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T036, T028e
- **Estimated Effort**: 4 days

## What Is Being Built
Facility management for factory, warehouse, and office buildings. Facility register with hierarchy (Building → Floor → Room → Zone). Space management and occupancy tracking. Utility metering (electricity, water, gas) with bill tracking and cost allocation to cost centres. Building maintenance schedules (cleaning, security, pest control, fire safety). Compliance tracking (inspections, certifications).

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/assets/models.py`):
  - `Facility`: id, code, name, type (building/floor/room/zone), parent FK (self — hierarchy), address, area_sqft, capacity, department FK (optional), cost_centre FK (optional), status (active/under_maintenance/retired), company FK, created_at
  - `FacilityMaintenanceSchedule`: id, facility FK, maintenance_type (cleaning/security/pest_control/fire_safety/inspection/other), frequency (daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly), scheduled_day_of_week, vendor FK (optional), assigned_team FK (optional), estimated_cost, is_active, company FK
  - `FacilityMaintenanceLog`: id, schedule FK, facility FK, scheduled_date, completed_date, vendor FK, cost, notes, status (scheduled/completed/cancelled), company FK
  - `UtilityMeter`: id, facility FK, utility_type (electricity/water/gas/telecom/other), meter_number, installation_date, is_active, company FK
  - `UtilityReading`: id, meter FK, reading_date, previous_reading, current_reading, consumption, cost_per_unit, total_cost, bill_reference, company FK
  - `UtilityCostAllocation`: id, reading FK, cost_centre FK, allocation_percent, allocated_amount
  - `ComplianceItem`: id, facility FK, compliance_type (fire_safety/electrical_inspection/structural_audit/environmental/other), description, frequency, last_inspection_date, next_inspection_date, certificate_number, certificate_expiry, responsible_person FK, vendor FK (optional), status (compliant/expired/overdue), company FK
- **Service** (`apps/assets/services/facility_service.py`):
  - `create_facility(data)` — create with hierarchy
  - `get_facility_tree(company)` — build facility hierarchy
  - `record_utility_reading(meter_id, reading)` — record reading, calculate consumption
  - `allocate_utility_cost(reading_id, allocations)` — allocate to cost centres
  - `get_utility_report(facility_id, period)` — consumption and cost trends
  - `get_compliance_status(company)` — all items with status
  - `get_overdue_compliance(company)` — expired/overdue items
  - `get_facility_cost_summary(company, period)` — total facility costs
- **API** (`apps/assets/api/facility_api.py`):
  - Facility CRUD, tree, utility CRUD, readings, allocations, compliance, reports

### Frontend (React)
- **Facility Tree Page**: hierarchical view of buildings/floors/rooms
- **Facility Detail Page**: space info, utility meters, compliance status, maintenance schedule
- **Utility Meter Page**: meter list, reading entry form
- **Utility Report Page**: consumption and cost charts by facility/period
- **Compliance Dashboard**: all compliance items with status, overdue alerts
- **Facility Cost Summary**: total costs by facility, allocation breakdown
