# T038 — Capital Allowance Schedules

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T032
- **Estimated Effort**: 3 days

## What Is Being Built
Schedule 3 ITA 1967 capital allowance computation: Initial Allowance (IA) + Annual Allowance (AA). Category-based rates (heavy machinery, motor vehicle, office equipment, etc.). Notional allowance on disposal. Separate from book depreciation.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/assets/models.py`):
  - `CapitalAllowanceSchedule`: fixed_asset FK, year, initial_allowance, annual_allowance, total_allowance, cumulative_allowance, residual_expenditure, pool_code
  - `CapitalAllowancePool`: pool_code, name, description, rate_percent, company FK
- **Service**: calculate IA (one-time at purchase), calculate AA (annual), notional allowance on disposal, generate CA report
- **API**: CA schedule per asset, CA report by pool, CA computation for tax return

### Capital Allowance Rates (seeded)
| Asset Category | Rate | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy Machinery | 14% | AA |
| Motor Vehicle | 20% | AA |
| Office Equipment | 20% | AA |
| Computer & IT | 40% | AA |
| Furniture & Fittings | 10% | AA |
| Building & Structure | 3% | AA |
