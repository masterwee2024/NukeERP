# T059 — Sales Quotation

- **Phase**: 5
- **Depends On**: T014, T041, T055
- **Estimated Effort**: 4 days

## What Is Being Built
`SalesQuotation` CRUD with multi-line items. Item lookup from catalog. Discount per line/header. Tax per line. Validity period. Convert to Sales Order. Quotation print/email (PDF).

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/crm/models.py`):
  - `SalesQuotation`: quotation_number, customer FK, quotation_date, valid_until_date, status (draft/sent/accepted/rejected/expired), discount_percent (header), total, discount_total, tax_total, grand_total, currency, terms_conditions, notes, company FK
  - `SalesQuotationLine`: quotation FK, line_number, item FK, description, quantity, uom FK, unit_price, discount_percent, amount, tax_code FK, tax_amount
- **Service**: CRUD, tax calculation, convert to SO, PDF generation
- **API**: Full CRUD + convert + print endpoints
