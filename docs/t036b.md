# T036b — Asset Insurance

- **Phase**: 3
- **Depends On**: T036
- **Estimated Effort**: 2 days

## What Is Being Built
Asset insurance policy tracking. Record insurance policies covering assets, premium payments, coverage amounts, policy dates, and renewal reminders. Link assets to insurance policies. Insurance claim tracking when assets are damaged.

## How It Will Be Built

### Backend (Django)
- **Models** (`apps/assets/models.py`):
  - `InsurancePolicy`: id, policy_number, insurer, policy_type (fire/theft/machinery/all-risk), coverage_amount, premium_amount, premium_frequency (monthly/quarterly/yearly), start_date, end_date, excess_amount, status (active/expired/cancelled), company FK, created_at
  - `AssetInsurance`: asset FK, policy FK, insured_value, unique(asset, policy)
  - `InsuranceClaim`: id, asset FK, policy FK, claim_number, incident_date, description, claimed_amount, approved_amount, status (submitted/investigating/approved/rejected/paid), settlement_date, company FK, created_at
- **Service** (`apps/assets/services/insurance_service.py`):
  - `create_policy(data)` — create insurance policy
  - `link_assets(policy_id, asset_ids)` — link assets to policy
  - `get_expiring_policies(days)` — policies expiring within N days
  - `create_claim(data)` — create insurance claim
  - `get_asset_coverage(asset_id)` — check if asset is insured and coverage amount
- **API** (`apps/assets/api/insurance_api.py`):
  - Policy CRUD, asset linking, claim CRUD, expiring policies alert

### Frontend (React)
- **Policy List Page**: all policies with status, expiry date, coverage
- **Policy Form Page**: create/edit policy, link assets
- **Claim List Page**: all claims with status
- **Claim Form Page**: create/edit claim
- **Expiring Policies Widget**: dashboard widget showing policies expiring soon
